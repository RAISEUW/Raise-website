  in AI and their impacts. Her project, “AI Ageism: New Forms of Age
  Discrimination and Exclusion in the Era of Algorithms and Artificial
  Intelligence” (AGEAI), examines how AI technologies affect aging populations
  in Europe from a socio-technical perspective.

  In this talk, Dr. Stypińska will present findings from her AGEAI research on
  AI ageism, supported by empirical data from Germany, Spain, Great Britain,
  Poland, and the Netherlands. She will also reflect on how the social sciences
  can provide a deeper and more systematic understanding of algorithmic
  inequalities, extending beyond the study of AI bias.

title: Context and Participation in Machine Learning
speaker: Guest Speaker
date: 2024-04-12T07:00:00.000Z
abstract: >-
  In AI & ML, participatory approaches hold promise to lend agency and
  decision-making power to marginalized stakeholders. But what does meaningful
  participation look like in practice? This talk will first cover an in-depth
  case study of designing ML tools with and in service of activists who monitor
  gender-related violence. Drawing from intersectional feminist theory and
  participatory design, we develop methods for data collection, annotation,
  modeling, and evaluation that aim to prioritize activist expertise and
  sustainable partnerships. Then, we’ll consider what participatory approaches
  should look like in the age of foundation models.

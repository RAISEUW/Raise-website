abstract: >-
  The rapid rise of artificial intelligence brings challenges in ensuring it
  aligns with human values and ethics. This session dives into the philosophical
  core of AI Alignment—how to build systems that act in line with human
  intentions and morals. Topics include value uncertainty, ethical
  decision-making, existential risks, and approaches like Inverse Reinforcement
  Learning, Coherent Extrapolated Volition, and Constitutional AI. Beyond
  technical fixes, AI Alignment raises profound questions about intelligence,
  ethics, and the future of human-technology interaction.

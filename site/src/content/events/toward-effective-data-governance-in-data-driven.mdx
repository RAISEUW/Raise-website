abstract: >-
  Contemporary AI systems are characterized by extensive personal data
  collection despite the increasing societal costs associated with such
  practices. To prevent harm, data protection regulations specify various
  principles for respectfully processing user data, such as purpose limitation,
  data minimization, or the need to establish a valid basis for data processing,
  such as consent or legitimate interest. Yet, practical implementations of
  these principles in data-driven systems leave much to be desired. This talk
  will delve into the computational and human factors contributing to lax data
  protection implementations and uncover ways research can contribute to
  improved data governance online.

abstract: >-
  Data is the lifeblood of AI. However, much of the most valuable data in the
  nation requires tight access control due to its sensitivity. As a result, AI
  remains heavily underutilized in some of the most important domains, including
  healthcare and genomics. In this talk, Sikha will discuss promising solutions
  to overcome this data bottleneck while safeguarding privacy. She will share
  insights into ongoing research on cryptographic techniques and the generation
  of synthetic data using the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR).

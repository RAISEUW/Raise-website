abstract: >-
  With the widespread proliferation of machine learning, there arises both the
  opportunity for societal benefit as well as the risk of harm. Approaching
  responsible machine learning is challenging because technical approaches may
  prioritize a mathematical definition of fairness that correlates poorly to
  real-world constructs of fairness due to too many layers of abstraction.
  Conversely, social approaches that engage with prescriptive theories may
  produce findings that are too abstract to effectively translate into practice.
  In my research, I bridge these approaches and utilize social implications to
  guide technical work.

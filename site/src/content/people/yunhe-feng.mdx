Yunhe Feng is a Postdoctoral Scholar with Professor Chirag Shah in the Information School at the University of Washington. Yunhe obtained his Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, USA. His research interest lies in Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery, Mobile Usable Security and Privacy, Machine Learning, and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI).

Muhammad Aurangzeb Ahmad is Resident Fellow at the Harborview Medical Center (UW Medicine) and an Affiliate adjunct faculty in the Department of Computer Science at University of Washington (UW) in Bothell. His research focuses on responsible AI (especially fairness, robustness and bias) in healthcare, digital twins in healthcare and personality emulation.

AI for All

RAISE Pop-up Hack Day

A hands-on day of AI projects in the public interest — students, professionals, faculty, postdocs, and staff all welcome. Work on a curated civic-tech project or bring your own.

When
December 3rd · 1–4 PM
Where
4545 Building, Room 122
4545 15th Ave NE, Seattle — 1st-floor common space, corner of 15th & 45th
Bring
A laptop
We provide coffee, pastries, the UW Purple low-code platform, cloud resources, and expert help
RSVP & agenda ↗ Submit a project idea ↗

Hosted by the AI Clinic, sponsored by RAISE and affiliated partners. The hack day focuses on civic tech and .gov projects: rapidly prototyping AI systems for government, experimenting with new modeling approaches, and creating baselines for collaborative work with the City of Seattle and public-sector partners.

RAISE is the University of Washington's hub for responsible AI research, education, and public-sector innovation — partnering with government agencies, nonprofits, and communities to design, evaluate, and deploy AI systems that improve public services while upholding standards of transparency, safety, and equity.

Curated projects4
PROJECT 01 · CITY OF SEATTLE

Customer Service Assistant

Design and test an AI assistant that answers resident questions about City services with multilingual, plain-language responses — prompt design, retrieval augmentation, and evaluation against curated resident questions.

Project brief ↗
PROJECT 02 · CITY OF SEATTLE

Policy Conflict Engine

Prototype search, retrieval, and model-based comparison workflows that surface conflicts, redundancies, and outdated requirements across Seattle's regulatory sources — WAC, SMC, Director's Rules, and Executive Orders.

Project brief ↗
PROJECT 03 · GLOBAL HEALTH

Streamlining Inference of Cause of Death

Use LLMs to predict cause of death from verbal autopsy interviews, then approximate expert "decode panels" with coordinated LLM agents — establishing baselines for expert diagnostic reasoning in regions without complete medical records.

Project brief ↗
PROJECT 04 · HEALTHCARE

Information Extraction from Healthcare-Bill Collections

Design prompts and few-shot strategies to extract key fields from messy legal PDFs in civil suits over unpaid medical bills — principal amount, judgment amount, defendant, and provider — evaluated against gold-standard answers.
